Article 1: Scope
This policy governs business trips by employees of ACME Inc., meaning
work performed temporarily away from the usual place of work.

Article 2: Trip authorization
Every trip must be authorized in advance by the function manager through
the internal form, stating the destination, duration, and purpose.

Article 3: Use of a personal car
Using a personal car for business reasons is allowed only with the
manager's written authorization. Without authorization, the employee must
use public transport or company cars.

Article 4: Mileage reimbursement
For authorized use of a personal car, a mileage reimbursement of 0.42
euros per kilometer is granted, calculated on the shortest route between
the place of work and the trip destination.

Article 5: Tolls and parking
On top of the mileage reimbursement in Article 4, highway tolls and
parking expenses are reimbursed when documented with a receipt.

Article 6: Meal reimbursement in Italy
For trips within the country, meal reimbursement is granted up to 50 euros
per day, on presentation of receipts.

Article 7: Meal reimbursement abroad
For trips abroad, the daily cap in Article 6 is raised to 80 euros.

Article 8: Overnight stays
Overnight stays are reimbursed at cost, in accommodations rated no higher
than four stars, on presentation of an invoice made out to the employee.
