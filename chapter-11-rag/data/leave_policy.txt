Article 1: Leave accrual
Each employee accrues 26 working days of leave per year of service, in
proportion to the months actually worked.

Article 2: Period of use
Accrued leave must normally be taken by June 30 of the year following the
year in which it was accrued.

Article 3: Leave request
Leave is requested through the internal portal and is subject to approval
by the function manager, who takes the department's operational needs into
account.

Article 4: Notice
For leave periods of up to one week the minimum notice is 10 working days;
for periods of two weeks or more the minimum notice is 30 working days.

Article 5: Paid time off
Employees are entitled to paid time off for medical appointments,
bereavement, and documented family reasons, within the limits set by the
applicable collective agreement.
