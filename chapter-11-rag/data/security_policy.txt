Article 1: Passwords
Company passwords must be at least 12 characters long, unique for each
service, and stored in the company password manager. Sharing them or
writing them down in plain text is prohibited.

Article 2: Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication is mandatory for every company service that
supports it, starting with email and the VPN.

Article 3: Personal devices
Access to company systems from personal devices is allowed only from
devices registered with the IT department and equipped with the company
security profile.

Article 4: Incident reporting
Every suspected security incident, including phishing emails and the loss
of company devices, must be reported within 24 hours to the IT department
through the dedicated channel.
